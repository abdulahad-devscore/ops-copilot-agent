# Cycle Counting

## Frequency
- **A items**: counted monthly at the DC and every two weeks at stores.
- **B items**: quarterly.
- **C items**: twice a year.
- Raw materials at the factory: aluminium ingot and coating weekly (high value), packaging monthly.

## Procedure
1. Odoo generates the count list (Inventory → Physical Inventory) on the first working day of the week.
2. Counters count blind (system quantity hidden).
3. If the count differs from system by more than **2%** or more than 5 units, a second person recounts.
4. The supervisor applies the adjustment in Odoo.

## Approval of adjustments
- Adjustment value up to RM 1,000: Warehouse Manager.
- Above RM 1,000: Warehouse Manager and Finance Controller.
- Any adjustment on small appliances (theft risk) is reported to the Head of Retail regardless of value.

## Root cause
Recurring variances on the same SKU (three months in a row) trigger a root-cause review: receiving errors, unrecorded damages, picking errors, or theft.
