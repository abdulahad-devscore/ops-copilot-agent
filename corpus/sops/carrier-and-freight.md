# Carriers, Freight and Delivery SLAs

## Carriers
| Lane | Carrier | Service | SLA |
|---|---|---|---|
| DC → KLCC / PJ stores | Own truck (2 x 3-tonne) | Scheduled Tue/Fri | Next morning |
| DC → Penang store | Lintas Utara Haulage | Line-haul Mon/Thu | 2 days |
| Wholesale, Klang Valley | Own truck | Daily runs | 1–2 working days |
| Wholesale, northern & southern | Lintas Utara Haulage / Selatan Express | Pallet | 3 working days |
| East Malaysia | Borneo Sea Link (sea freight via Port Klang) | FCL/LCL | 7–10 days port to port |
| Online parcels | ParcelGo | Courier | 1–4 working days |
| Imports from China | Forwarder: Seri Samudera Logistics | FOB Shenzhen/Guangzhou | 14–18 days sea + 3 days customs |

## Freight cost guide
- Pallet to Penang: RM 120 per pallet.
- Courier emergency crate within Klang Valley: RM 45.
- Sea freight LCL to Kuching: RM 210 per cubic metre.

## Claims
Damaged-in-transit claims must be filed with the carrier within **3 days** with photos and the signed delivery note. ParcelGo liability is capped at RM 200 per parcel unless declared value insurance was bought.
