# Damaged Goods and Quarantine

- Every warehouse and store has a **QUARANTINE** location in Odoo. Damaged, returned-used or failed-QC items are moved there immediately; they are not available for sale.
- Stores send quarantine stock to the DC returns cage every **Friday** on the replenishment truck.
- The DC reviews the cage every Monday: supplier claim (manufacturing defects on imported goods), rework (factory items), or scrap.
- Scrapping more than RM 500 of stock in one go needs the Warehouse Manager's approval; above RM 2,000 also the Finance Controller.
- Supplier claims for Brightwave appliances are batched monthly with photos and serial numbers.
