# Wholesale Order to Cash

1. **Order entry**: the sales executive enters the customer's PO as a quotation in Odoo under the Wholesale team, warehouse DC, quoting the customer's PO number.
2. **Credit check**: before confirming, check the customer is not on credit hold (over limit, or anything 60+ days overdue). On hold → Finance decides; see the Customer Credit Policy.
3. **Confirm**: confirming reserves stock at the DC. If stock is short, offer a split delivery; backorders ship automatically when stock arrives.
4. **Pick, pack, dispatch**: Klang Valley orders ship within 1–2 working days; northern/southern within 3; East Malaysia per the sea-freight schedule.
5. **Invoice**: Finance invoices the day after dispatch. Invoices quote the customer's PO number and our DO number.
6. **Collect**: follow the collections timeline (7 / 21 / 45 / 60 days).

Target order-to-cash cycle: 35 days for 30-day-term customers.
