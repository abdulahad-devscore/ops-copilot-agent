# Online Order Fulfilment at the DC

1. Online orders flow into Odoo every 15 minutes as sales orders under the **Online** sales team, shipping from the Port Klang DC.
2. Pick waves run at 09:00, 11:30 and 15:00. Orders confirmed before 12:00 must ship the same working day.
3. Packing standards: cookware in double-wall cartons; glass tumbler sets with honeycomb inserts; appliances in the original carton plus an outer shipper.
4. ParcelGo collects at 16:30. Tracking numbers are written back to the order automatically.
5. If an item is out of stock at the DC, customer service offers a 3-day backorder or a refund. Do not pull stock from stores for online orders without the E-commerce Lead's approval.

Service levels: 98% of orders shipped on time; fewer than 0.5% damaged in transit.
