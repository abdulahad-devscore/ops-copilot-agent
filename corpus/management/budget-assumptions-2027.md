# 2027 Budget Assumptions (draft, management only)

- Revenue growth: wholesale +7%, retail stores +4%, online +25%.
- List price increase of 5% on cookware from 1 February 2027 to offset aluminium costs (wholesale customers to be notified by 1 January).
- Aluminium ingot assumed at RM 11.80/kg (current contract RM 11.20/kg).
- Headcount: +2 at the DC (pick/pack for online), +1 production planner.
- Capex: second injection-moulding machine (RM 380,000) in Q2 2027 to double storage-item capacity.
- Air fryer volume forecast +40% year on year; negotiate a second appliance supplier as backup to Brightwave.
