# QC Inspection Spec: Lunch Box 3-compartment (TH-KS-4220)

Sampling: AQL 4.0, General Inspection Level II, per production lot.

| Check | Method | Accept |
|---|---|---|
| Dimensions | Calipers / tape vs drawing TH-KS-4220-D1 | ±1.0 mm |
| Visual | Under 1,000 lux at 40 cm | No pits, bubbles or coating peel |
| Seal leak test | Water inversion 30 s | No leak |
| Packaging | Barcode scan, carton count | 24 per carton, EAN readable |

Critical defects (sharp edges, loose handles, cracked lids) reject the lot. Rework is allowed once; the second failure
means scrap and a corrective action report to the Production Manager.
