# Category Guide: Cookware

Woks and stockpots are A items and festival best sellers; frying pans and saucepans are B items.

SKUs: TH-CW-2401 Cast Aluminium Wok 32cm, TH-CW-2402 Cast Aluminium Wok 36cm, TH-CW-2410 Non-stick Frying Pan 24cm, TH-CW-2411 Non-stick Frying Pan 28cm, TH-CW-2420 Stockpot 24cm with Glass Lid, TH-CW-2421 Stockpot 28cm with Glass Lid, TH-CW-2430 Saucepan 18cm, TH-CW-2440 Grill Pan 28cm.

Master carton: 6 units. Care: Hand wash with a soft sponge. Use wooden or silicone utensils. Never heat an empty non-stick pan on high heat. Season cast aluminium woks with a thin film of oil after washing.
