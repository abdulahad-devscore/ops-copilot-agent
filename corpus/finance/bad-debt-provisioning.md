# Bad Debt Provisioning and Write-off

Provision is booked at month end on overdue trade receivables:

| Days past due | Provision |
|---|---|
| 1–60 | 0% |
| 61–90 | 10% |
| 91–120 | 25% |
| 121–180 | 50% |
| Over 180 | 100% |

Write-offs need the Managing Director's approval and are only made after external collections have closed the case. Amounts recovered after write-off are booked as other income.

The provision report is generated from Odoo's aged receivables (Accounting → Reporting → Aged Receivable) on the first working day of the month.
