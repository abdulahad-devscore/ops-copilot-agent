# Gross Margin by Channel and Category (FY2026 year to date, Jan–Sep)

| Channel | Revenue share | Gross margin |
|---|---|---|
| Wholesale | 86% | 38% |
| Retail stores | 9% | 61% |
| Online | 5% | 57% (after shipping subsidies) |

| Category | Gross margin (all channels) |
|---|---|
| Cookware (made in-house) | 52% |
| Bakeware (made in-house) | 58% |
| Kitchen storage (made in-house) | 64% |
| Tableware (imported) | 55% |
| Small appliances (imported) | 34% |

Notes:
- Small appliances carry the lowest margin; the Deepavali air fryer promotion at RM 279 cuts its margin to about 27% at retail.
- Aluminium ingot prices rose 6% in Q3; cookware margin is expected to fall by roughly 2 points in Q4 unless list prices change.
