# Month-end Close Checklist

Working day 1:
- All receipts and deliveries validated in Odoo (Warehouse Manager confirms).
- Vendor bills from Invoice Flow posted; AP review queue empty or carried over with reasons.
- Bank reconciliation for Maybank and CIMB accounts.

Working day 2:
- Inventory valuation report vs general ledger; investigate differences above RM 500.
- Bad-debt provision booked from the aged receivables report.
- Accruals for goods received not invoiced (GRNI).

Working day 3:
- Management accounts pack to the Managing Director: P&L by channel, gross margin by category, cash flow.
