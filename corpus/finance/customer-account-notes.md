# Wholesale Customer Account Notes (confidential)

## Dapur Bonda Trading (CUS-DBT)
Small kitchenware shop chain in Selangor (3 outlets). Chronic late payer: pays 15–25 days after due date on average. A **payment plan of RM 5,000 per month** was agreed on 2 September 2026 to clear the older balance. Credit limit RM 20,000. Do not increase the limit until two consecutive months are paid on time. Owner contact prefers WhatsApp.

## Kuching Home Centre Sdn Bhd (CUS-KHC)
East Malaysia distributor on 45-day terms. Pays late during the school holidays because of their own cash-flow cycle; historically always pays in full. Credit limit RM 60,000. Sea-freight delays occasionally cause disputed short deliveries; check the DC dispatch photos before raising a credit note.

## Restoran Selera Kampung Group (CUS-RSK)
Restaurant group (5 outlets) on 14-day terms, credit limit RM 12,000. Has had two bounced cheques in 2025. Finance recommends **cash on delivery for any order above RM 5,000**.

## Pasaraya Seri Mutiara Sdn Bhd (CUS-PSM)
Largest wholesale account (supermarket chain, 22 branches). Pays reliably via bank transfer on day 28–32. Credit limit RM 150,000. Their AP team sometimes holds a single invoice when a delivery note is missing; resend the signed DO rather than escalating.

## Borneo Living Retail Sdn Bhd (CUS-BLR)
Sabah home-store chain, 45-day terms, credit limit RM 120,000. Good payer. Requested a limit increase to RM 150,000 for the year-end season (25%); this needs the Finance Controller **and** the Managing Director under the credit policy.

## Hotel Cempaka Kuala Lumpur (CUS-HCK)
Buys cookware and tableware for its kitchens and banquet hall twice a quarter. Credit limit RM 40,000. Pays through a procurement portal; invoices must quote the hotel PO number.
