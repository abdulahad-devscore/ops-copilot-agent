# Pricing and Discount Authority

| Who | Maximum extra discount (on top of standard terms) |
|---|---|
| Store staff | 5% on a single item for damaged packaging |
| Store manager | 10% per transaction, maximum RM 300 |
| Sales executive (wholesale) | 2% per order |
| Head of Sales | 5% per order |
| Managing Director | Above 5% |

Rules:
- Never sell below cost without Finance approval.
- Campaign prices (e.g. Deepavali) cannot be combined with staff discounts.
- Staff purchase discount is 20% off list, up to RM 1,000 per year, excluding small appliances during campaigns.
