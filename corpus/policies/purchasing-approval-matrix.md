# Purchasing Approval Matrix

## Scope
Applies to every purchase order (PO) raised in Odoo for raw materials, packaging, traded goods and indirect spend. Agent-proposed POs from the Ops Copilot follow the same matrix: the copilot can only *propose*; a person approves on the ERP Bridge approval page.

## Approval thresholds (value before SST)
| PO value | Approval required |
|---|---|
| Up to RM 10,000 | Operations Manager |
| RM 10,000.01 to RM 50,000 | Operations Manager and Finance Controller |
| Above RM 50,000 | Managing Director |

Splitting a requirement into several POs to stay under a threshold is a policy breach.

## Approved suppliers
Raw materials and packaging may only be bought from suppliers on the Approved Supplier List (see Supplier Profiles). Current approved suppliers:
- Aluminium ingot: Perak Alloy Supplies (SUP-PAS)
- Non-stick coating: Selangor Coatings (SUP-SCS)
- Resin, silicone seals, bakelite handles: Klang Valley Plastics (SUP-KVP)
- Glass lids and glass tumblers: Johor Glassworks (SUP-JGW)
- Cartons and sleeves: Penang Packaging (SUP-PPK)
- Carbon steel sheet: Butterworth Steel Trading (SUP-BST)
- Melamine tableware and cutlery: Hengfa Tableware Trading (SUP-HTT)
- Small appliances: Brightwave Home Appliances (SUP-BHA)

A new supplier needs a supplier evaluation (quality sample, factory audit for food-contact items, financial check) before its first PO.

## Urgent purchases
If a stock-out would stop the production line within 48 hours, the Operations Manager may approve a PO up to RM 50,000 alone and inform the Finance Controller the same day.

## What every PO must state
Receiving warehouse (FAC for raw materials and packaging, DC for traded goods), expected delivery date, and the reason (reorder rule, sales order, production plan).
