# Retail and Online Returns Policy

## Change of mind
Customers may return unused items in original packaging within **14 days** of purchase with a receipt. Tembusu Rewards members get **30 days**. Refunds go back to the original payment method; cash purchases over RM 500 are refunded by bank transfer within 5 working days.

## Small electrical appliances
Kettles, rice cookers, hand blenders and air fryers (TH-SA range) cannot be returned for change of mind once the seal is broken. If an appliance is defective within **7 days** of purchase, the store exchanges it on the spot. After 7 days, defects are handled under the **12-month manufacturer warranty** through Brightwave Home Appliances; the store logs the claim and ships the unit to the DC returns cage.

## Cookware warranty
Tembusu-made cookware carries a **2-year warranty on the non-stick coating** and a **5-year warranty on cast aluminium bodies** against warping under normal domestic use. Damage from metal utensils, dishwasher use on non-stick items, or empty-pan overheating is not covered.

## Online orders
Online customers may return within 14 days by booking a free pickup (West Malaysia) through the customer service form. East Malaysia customers return by post and are refunded the postage up to RM 25.

## Store process
1. Check receipt or e-receipt and purchase date in Odoo.
2. Inspect the item. Sellable items go back to store stock; damaged or used items go to the store's quarantine shelf and are transferred to the DC returns cage every Friday with the regular replenishment truck.
3. Process the refund or exchange. Refunds above RM 1,000 need the store manager's approval.

## Wholesale returns
Wholesale customers report damaged or short-shipped goods within 7 days of delivery. Finance raises a credit note after the DC confirms the claim; see the Customer Credit Policy for disputes.
