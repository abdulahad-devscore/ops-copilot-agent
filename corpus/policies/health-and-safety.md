# Warehouse and Factory Health and Safety Rules

- Safety shoes and high-visibility vests are mandatory on the factory floor and in the DC. Heat-resistant gloves and face shields at the casting line and cure oven.
- Only staff with a valid **forklift licence** (renewed every 2 years) may operate forklifts or reach trucks. Pedestrian walkways are marked in green.
- Maximum pallet stacking height at the DC: **3 pallets** for cookware, **4 pallets** for storage items.
- Near misses must be reported in the HSE log within the same shift. Lost-time incidents are reported to the Managing Director within 2 hours and to DOSH as required.
- Fire drills twice a year at each site; the last DC drill was on 12 August 2026.
