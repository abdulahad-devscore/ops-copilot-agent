## Scorecard
Suppliers are scored quarterly on on-time-in-full (target **95%**), quality rejects (target below **0.5%**), and invoice accuracy (target **98%** of invoices matching first time). Two consecutive quarters below target triggers a corrective action plan; three triggers re-sourcing.

## Known issues (Q3 2026)
- Klang Valley Plastics delivered only 80% of the October resin order on time; balance promised within 10 days.
- Hengfa partial shipment on the mid-October tableware order; remaining quantity on the next vessel.
- Brightwave air fryer demand is trending up; procurement agreed to place orders every 3 weeks instead of monthly.
