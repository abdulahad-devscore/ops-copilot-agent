# Customer Credit Policy

## Purpose
This policy sets how Tembusu Housewares extends trade credit to wholesale customers (supermarkets, kitchen shops, hotels and restaurant groups). Online and retail store sales are paid at the point of sale and are out of scope.

## Payment terms
Standard terms are 30 days from invoice date. Borneo Living Retail and Kuching Home Centre have 45-day terms because of sea-freight delivery times to East Malaysia. Restaurant groups are on 14-day terms. Any other terms must be approved by the Finance Controller and recorded on the customer master in Odoo.

## Credit limits
Every wholesale customer has a credit limit recorded in Odoo. New customers start at RM 10,000 for the first three months. Limits are reviewed every six months against payment history.

| Limit increase requested | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 20% of current limit | Finance Controller |
| More than 20%, up to RM 50,000 total limit | Finance Controller and Managing Director |
| Above RM 50,000 total limit | Managing Director, with a credit report |

## Credit hold
A customer is placed on credit hold automatically when either condition is true:

1. The open receivable is above the credit limit, or
2. Any invoice is 60 days or more past its due date.

While on hold, new sales orders may be entered but must not be confirmed or shipped. The Finance Controller may release a single order from hold if the customer pays at least 50% of the overdue balance or agrees a written payment plan.

## Collections timeline
- 7 days overdue: automated reminder email from Odoo.
- 21 days overdue: phone call by the account's sales executive, logged in Odoo chatter.
- 45 days overdue: formal letter of demand from Finance; sales executive informs the customer that hold will apply at 60 days.
- 60 days overdue: credit hold (see above).
- 120 days overdue: referral to external collections; 50% bad-debt provision.

## Disputes
If a customer disputes an invoice (wrong quantity, damaged goods), the disputed amount is excluded from the overdue calculation for up to 30 days while a credit note is assessed. Disputes must be logged with the invoice number.
