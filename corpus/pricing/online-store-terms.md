# Online Store Terms and Shipping

Online prices equal retail list prices unless a campaign is running.

## Shipping
- West Malaysia: **free shipping for orders of RM 150 and above**; otherwise RM 9.90 flat.
- East Malaysia: RM 25 flat, regardless of order value.
- Orders are packed at the Port Klang DC. Orders placed before 12:00 on a working day ship the same day.

## Delivery times
Klang Valley 1–2 working days; rest of Peninsular Malaysia 2–4 working days; East Malaysia 5–7 working days.

## Vouchers and stacking
Only one voucher per order. Vouchers cannot be combined with campaign prices on small appliances.
