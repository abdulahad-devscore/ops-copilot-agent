# Wholesale Trading Terms 2026

## Trade discount
Wholesale customers buy at **15% off the retail list price**. Orders above **RM 20,000** (before SST) get an extra **3% volume discount** on the whole order. Hotels and restaurants (HoReCa) get the same terms.

## Minimum order
RM 1,500 per order. Cookware in master cartons of 6, storage items in cartons of 24, melamine in cartons of 48.

## Delivery
- Free delivery within the Klang Valley for orders above RM 2,000; otherwise RM 80 per drop.
- Northern region (Penang, Kedah, Perak): free above RM 5,000, delivered within 3 working days.
- East Malaysia (Sabah, Sarawak): ex-Port Klang; sea freight arranged at cost, 7–10 days transit.

## Payment
30 days from invoice date unless otherwise agreed (see Customer Credit Policy). Prices exclude 10% SST.

## Price changes
Tembusu gives wholesale customers **30 days' written notice** of list price changes. Confirmed orders keep the price at confirmation.
